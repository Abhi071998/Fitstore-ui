# FITstore — What It Is (Product Overview)

This document explains FITstore in plain language — what it does and what a shopper can do on it. No technical background needed.

## What is FITstore?

FITstore is an online clothing store. Shoppers can browse t-shirts and apparel organized by category, look at product details, add items to a shopping bag, and place an order — much like any familiar online shopping site.

## Who uses it?

- **Customers** — anyone visiting the site to browse and buy.
- **Store staff (separately, not covered by this app)** — someone on the business side reviews and approves orders after they come in. This app doesn't include that approval screen; it's the customer-facing storefront only.

## What can a customer do?

**1. Browse without an account.**
Anyone can look around freely — no sign-up required just to window-shop. The home page shows a hero banner, some promotional messaging, and a set of shopping categories to click into. Clicking a category shows every product in it; clicking a product shows a full detail page — photos, price, description, and the available sizes with how many are in stock.

**2. Create an account or log in.**
A "Login / Sign Up" option sits in the top-right of every page. This is required before adding anything to the bag or placing an order — browsing itself never requires it.

**3. Add items to the bag.**
On a product's page, the customer picks a size (or several sizes at once, with a quantity for each) and adds them to their bag. If an item is out of stock, that's shown clearly and can't be added.

**4. Review and adjust the bag.**
The bag page lists everything added, with the price breakdown (item total, any savings from a discount, and the final total). A customer can remove an item entirely, or open a small popup to change the quantity of an item up or down before confirming the change.

**5. Check out.**
From the bag, "Proceed to Checkout" leads to a shipping form — name and email are already filled in from the account, and the customer types in their delivery address. Placing the order shows an on-screen confirmation with an order number and the shipping details that were submitted.

**6. View order history.**
A logged-in customer has an "Orders" icon in the top navigation leading to a full list of past orders — what was ordered, the delivery address used, the total paid, and a status for each order (for example, "Pending Approval" while the store is still reviewing it). If an item within an order ends up cancelled, that's shown clearly on that order too.

**7. Read About Us.**
An "About Us" page describes the store — its story, some taglines about what it stands for (fair pricing, quality), when it was established, its address, and a contact email.

## What happens after an order is placed?

Placing an order is not the final step for the business — it goes into a **pending approval** state. Someone on the store's side reviews it before it's confirmed further. There is currently no online payment step built into this app; that also happens outside of what a customer sees here.

## What's not part of this yet

- No way to search for a product by typing a name — browsing is by category only.
- No online payment collection.
- No way for a customer to cancel their own order or an item in it from this app (the store's side has to be involved).
- No saved/multiple delivery addresses — every checkout starts with a blank address form.

## A typical customer journey, start to finish

1. Land on the home page, browse categories.
2. Click into a category, then into a specific product.
3. Pick a size and quantity, add to bag.
4. Realize they want another item too — repeat step 2–3.
5. Open the bag, notice they want one fewer of an item — adjust the quantity in the popup.
6. Proceed to checkout, fill in the delivery address (name/email already filled in).
7. Place the order, see the confirmation screen.
8. Come back later, check "Orders" to see the order is now approved (or still pending).
